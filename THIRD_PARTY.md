# Third-party assets

Vendor glyphs in `app/src/main/res/drawable/ic_vendor_*.xml` are monochrome traces of the vendors' own marks. Trademarks belong to their owners and are used only to identify the model provider.

- [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons) (CC0-1.0): anthropic, gemini, meta, mistral, deepseek, qwen, moonshot, perplexity, minimax, bytedance, xiaomi, nvidia
- [Lobe Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons) (MIT, Copyright (c) LobeHub): openai, gemma, zai, xai, stepfun, cohere, microsoft
